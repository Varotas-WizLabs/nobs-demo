NobsAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

NobsAI («душнила AI») is a fact-checking service for YouTube videos, published by WizLabs (Varotas). This policy explains what NobsAI collects when you use it and how that data is handled. It is the WizLabs company policy applied to NobsAI.

1. What we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in with an e-mail link or with Google, we receive your e-mail address and, for Google, your name and profile picture as returned by Google (scopes openid, email, profile). No other Google data is requested.
  • Your requests. The YouTube links you submit, the analysis mode you chose (Light or Deep), the visibility you set for each video (Public, Unlisted or Private), and your credit balance and history.
  • Technical data needed to run the service: session tokens, timestamps and error logs.

We do not collect data about people other than the signed-in user.

2. How we use it

Data is used only to provide NobsAI: to sign you in, to run the analyses you request, to show you your own videos and balance, and to enforce the visibility you chose. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or resale, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.

3. Visibility of analyses

An analysis marked Public is shown on the public site. Unlisted analyses are available only through their direct link, and Private analyses only to you. You can change the visibility at any time on your account page. Analyses describe public YouTube videos; your e-mail address is never shown next to them.

4. Where it is stored

Accounts, requests and private analyses are stored in our Supabase project (EU, Ireland). Public analyses are published on GitHub Pages. Credentials are stored encrypted and are never shown in logs or interfaces.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the services needed to perform what you asked for (Supabase for accounts and storage, Google for Google sign-in, YouTube for the video you submitted), under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Google API Services

NobsAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can revoke NobsAI's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and you can ask us to delete your account and its data by writing to the address below.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, row-level access rules so that each user can read only their own records, and access to operational systems restricted to the people running the service.

9. Children

NobsAI is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above changes when we do.
